How Tooq works

Three simple steps between a lost pet and a happy reunion.

1

Tag your pet

Attach the Tooq tag to your pet's collar and link its PIN to their profile in the app.

2

Someone finds them

The finder enters the tag's PIN on Tooq and instantly sees your pet's public profile.

3

You're reunited

They contact you through the details you chose to share — you stay in control of your privacy.
