What's inside the app

More than a tag — TOOQ is a home for your pet's daily life.

Digital ID & TOOQ Tag

Every pet gets a profile. Link an optional TOOQ Tag and whoever finds them can open it with the tag's PIN — no account needed.

Health records

Vaccinations, vet visits, medications and weight — one tidy history you can share with your clinic.

Reminders & calendar

Never miss a booster, grooming day or medication dose. Upcoming care shows up right on your home screen.

TOOQ AI assistant

Ask about symptoms, food or behaviour and get helpful guidance grounded in your pet's own records.

Trusted access

Share a pet with family, a sitter or your vet. You stay the owner and decide exactly what they can see.

SOS & lost pet alerts

Mark your pet as lost with one tap. Finders see how to reach you, and sightings come straight back to you.

Found a pet wearing a TOOQ Tag?

You don't need an account. Open the app, tap I Found a Pet and enter the 4-digit PIN on the tag to see the pet's profile and let the owner know.
